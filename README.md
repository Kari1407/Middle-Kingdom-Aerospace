## Middle Kingdom Aerospace

Middle Kingdom Aerospace (MKA) is a new mod made by Kari and Questin focused on past, present and future launch vehicles from China. We aim to bring a wide variety of vehicles with high quality models and textures.


### Dependencies:
- B9 Part Switch
- Waterfall
- ModuleManager
- Community Resource Pack
- Shabby
- Simple Adjustable Fairings
- Animated Decouplers

### Recommended Mods:
- HullCameraVDS & NeptuneCamera
